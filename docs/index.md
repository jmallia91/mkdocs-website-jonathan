## About me

I'm Jonathan, a freelance **Software & AI Automation Developer**. I help small and medium-sized businesses and software companies turn repetitive processes and disconnected systems into efficient, automated workflows.

With **over 10 years of professional software-development experience** and a **Master's in Artificial Intelligence**, I combine traditional software engineering, integrations, APIs, and AI to build practical solutions that reduce manual work and improve how information moves through a business.

My background includes business applications, backend systems, databases, APIs, integrations, and enterprise software. That experience helps me work with the systems a business already depends on and introduce automation where it makes a useful difference.

### The business problem comes first

Many businesses are interested in AI but struggle to identify where it can provide practical value. My approach starts with understanding your process: where time is being lost, where information gets stuck, and what a better workflow would look like.

The solution might involve connecting systems through APIs, automating repetitive tasks, processing documents, adding AI to an existing application, or developing custom software. I choose the combination of tools that fits the problem, without tying the work to one automation platform or AI technology.
